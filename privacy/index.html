Privacy Policy — Game Pass Tracker

Last updated: August 2026

Game Pass Tracker ("the App") is an independent, unofficial companion app for Xbox Game Pass. This page explains what information the App handles.

1. No account, no personal data collection

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information. The App does not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. Data stored on your device

The App saves the following information locally on your device only, using standard Android app storage:

  • Your list of favorite games (title, cover image link, and related details)
  • A small internal list used to avoid repeating the same "leaving soon" notification twice

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server operated by us. It is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App.

3. Internet access and third-party services

The App uses your internet connection to fetch publicly available information from the following third-party services, in order to function:

  • Microsoft / Xbox — public Xbox Game Pass catalog data and Xbox Wire news, to show the game list and news feed.
  • RAWG.io — public game cover images for upcoming/leaving titles.
  • MyMemory Translation — automatic translation of news content when needed.
  • GitHub — a public data file maintained by the developer, used to keep the "upcoming/leaving games" list up to date.

These requests do not include any personal information about you. Standard technical information (such as your IP address) may be visible to these third-party services as part of any normal internet request, governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Notifications

The App may show local notifications on your device (for example, when a favorited game is about to leave Game Pass). These notifications are generated and shown entirely on your device; no push notification server is used.

5. Advertising and analytics

The App does not display ads and does not use any analytics or tracking SDKs.

6. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children, as it does not collect personal information from any user.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in the App. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest revision.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact: gamepasstracker@proton.me
